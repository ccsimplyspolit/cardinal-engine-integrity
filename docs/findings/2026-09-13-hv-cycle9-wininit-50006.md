## Why (user 23:51: не хукать системные DLL)

Identity NPT NX is **GPA**. Shared OS pages (ntdll/user32/kernelbase) are
the same PFN in every process.

1. **`arm_hijack_trap`** in `hypervisor.cpp` did `identity_epte->trap_execute()`
   on **ntdll** `NtWaitForSingleObject` / `NtDelayExecution`. That is a
   system-DLL hook. Cycle 9 hold used mailbox Relic RVAs, not ZPPI — but
   the ELF still contained the trap. **Now always returns false.**
2. **`stealth_arm` without `image_base`** could NX any user VA in the
   resolved CR3, including ntdll of that process, or a Relic-looking VA
   translated in the wrong DTB onto a shared OS page.
3. Nested **`-Verb RunAs`** from an already-elevated `Cycle9-HvOnly`
   flooded Appinfo after rbhost attach — matches wininit 0x50006
   (critical process / session death) even if NPT stayed on Relic
   `.text`.
